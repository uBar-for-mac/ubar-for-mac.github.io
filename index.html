uBar: The Dock Replacement

uBar replaces the Dock with a precise, configurable bar inspired by the Windows Taskbar. Tailored for macOS, it delivers a cleaner workflow whether you prefer a dock or taskbar experience.

Favorites at Your Fingertips

Pin your essential apps, Show Desktop, and Trash in a dedicated favorites area. Access them instantly without clutter.

Readable Application Badges

Unlike the Dock, uBar displays badges clearly—stay informed on notifications, updates, and activity without strain.

Multi-Row Flexibility

Drag the edge to expand up to five rows. Organize hundreds of apps efficiently on any display size.

Activity Mode

Hold Ctrl to reveal real-time CPU and memory usage for each app. Monitor performance effortlessly.

Smart Window Grouping

Group windows by application—choose Always, Never, or Automatic. Switch between instances with precision.

Custom Themes and Menu

Switch between Light and Dark themes. Tailor the uBar menu to match your workflow perfectly.

FAQ

Does uBar replace the default Dock?

Yes, it fully replaces the Dock while preserving all macOS functionality. Hide or show the original as needed.

Is it compatible with macOS Sequoia?

Version 4.2.3 includes fixes for menu positions on macOS 15.0 Sequoia and later.

Can I use both Light and Dark modes?

Switch effortlessly between themes to match your system appearance or preference.

How do I access app resource usage?

Hold the Ctrl key to enter Activity Mode and view CPU/memory stats for running apps.
